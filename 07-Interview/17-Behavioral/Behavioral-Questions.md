# Behavioral Questions

Record answers using the STAR method: **Situation → Task → Action → Result**. Keep concrete, metric-backed stories.

## Common themes
- A time you debugged a hard production issue.
- A disagreement with a teammate and how you resolved it.
- A project you're proud of and your specific contribution.
- A time you made a mistake and what you learned.
- How you handle competing priorities / tight deadlines.
- A time you improved performance, reliability, or process.
